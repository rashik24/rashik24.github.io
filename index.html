About

My work focuses on designing analytical and AI-enabled systems that support decision-makers operating under uncertainty, limited resources, fragmented information, and competing operational objectives.

I am particularly interested in problems where machine learning and optimization can be integrated to improve operational efficiency, resource accessibility, resilience, and equitable service delivery.

Selected Projects

Food Bank Purchasing and Distribution Optimization

Stochastic Optimization · Humanitarian Logistics

Developed optimization models for purchasing and allocating food across a multi-branch food bank network while accounting for supply uncertainty, demand variability, agency capacity, transportation, nutrition, inventory, and equity considerations.

Python Gurobi Stochastic Programming Optimization

Predicting Inter-Agency Collaboration and Resource Sharing

Machine Learning · Humanitarian Systems

Investigated operational and organizational factors associated with agencies' willingness to share excess food resources. The work uses predictive models and statistical analysis to identify patterns that may support more effective collaboration across humanitarian networks.

Machine Learning Logistic Regression Python Statistical Modeling

Geographic Accessibility of Food Assistance

Geospatial Analytics · Accessibility

Developed geospatial analyses of food assistance coverage to identify communities with limited geographic access to charitable food resources and evaluate disparities in service availability.

GIS Python Tableau Spatial Analysis

Resource Allocation Under Supply and Demand Uncertainty

Operations Research · Decision Support

Designed rolling-horizon and scenario-based optimization approaches for resource allocation when future donations, demand, purchasing requirements, and operational conditions are uncertain.

Stochastic Programming Rolling Horizon Gurobi Decision Support
